Before you use AI tools

Canvas Content Builder sends only the material you choose to process—your prompt, uploaded source files, or the Canvas content selected for an AI rewrite—through the Ingham ISD Google Apps Script proxy to the organization’s Airia AI service.

Your Canvas password and API key are not sent to the AI service. Do not submit student records, grades, IEP/504 information, passwords, API keys, or other confidential information unless your district has explicitly approved that use.

Review AI output before creating or publishing Canvas content. Google Drive links are not readable by the agent; upload/export a file or paste the relevant text instead.
